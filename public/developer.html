About the Developer

MD RAZA is the architect and full-stack software engineer behind WishCraft AI. Driven by a passion for human-centric software, MD RAZA envisioned a greeting platform where technological sophistication elevates emotional sincerity.

By combining generative intelligence with tactile micro-interactions (such as blowable birthday candles, wax-sealed letters, and emotion-aware audio), MD RAZA built an application that honors life's most meaningful relationships.

🪄

About WishCraft AI

WishCraft AI transforms standard text messages into memorable digital experiences. Whether celebrating birthdays, milestones, romantic anniversaries, or sincere apologies, each greeting is uniquely tailored with personalized memories and interactive visuals.

Every creation produces a unique shareable link that recipients can open on any smartphone, tablet, or browser worldwide.

Purpose & Vision

Connecting people through heartfelt digital expressions

In an era of disposable text messages, WishCraft AI restores the warmth, intentionality, and craftsmanship of physical greeting cards into the modern digital age.

Whether someone is celebrating a milestone birthday, honoring years of marriage, or saying a sincere apology, WishCraft AI ensures their words leave a lasting emotional impression.
